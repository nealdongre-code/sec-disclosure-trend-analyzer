Methodology & coverage

A selected sample

114 Exhibit 10 attachments from 19 issuers and 77 filings across technology, financial services, healthcare, industrials and energy. Coverage includes 2021, 2023 and 2025 filings on Forms 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K.

Healthcare/2021/8-K and energy/2025/8-K were not filled. The sample is purposive and is not representative of entire industries.

AI-prepared classifications

AI assigns broad primary categories, secondary tags and brief reasons during data preparation. Ambiguous records receive additional review. The classifications have not been independently accuracy-validated; confidence labels are judgments, not probabilities.

Visitor queries filter prepared metadata. No AI processing or live SEC scraping occurs in this dashboard.

What the counts mean

Counts measure exhibit attachments, including award forms, policies, amendments, restatements, addenda and bundled documents. They do not measure unique agreements, new transactions, transaction value or economic growth.

Filing dates are used, not agreement execution dates. Some records have source-version or date-consistency flags.

Sources & retention

Sources are publicly available EDGAR documents, retrieved under SEC fair-access conditions. Only metadata, classifications, short reasons and source links are retained in this dashboard. Full contract texts are not hosted.

Source availability may change. Always consult the original filing and appropriate qualified professionals.

Specific classification and date limitations

Eight records are intentionally in Other because the taxonomy does not separately classify indemnification, governance appointments, shareholder cooperation or personal aircraft use. Six records have medium-confidence labels. Primary category totals do not double-count secondary tags.

D076 and D077 preserve the SEC-published 2021 filing date while flagging a differing 2022 acceptance/accession year. Later source response hashes differed from the original study for ten records; this is not proof of a change in legal substance. The JSON download preserves provenance flags.
